# Garden Helper user guide

## First steps
1. **Garden profile** — name your garden and enter your typical last spring and first autumn frost. Sowing windows and alerts depend on them. (Or use **Site, sun & climate** to derive them from measured temperatures.)
2. **Site, sun & climate** — enter coordinates (decimal degrees, e.g. 46.7712 and 23.6236). *Save and calculate sun* works offline. *Fetch satellite picture and climate* asks for confirmation and then contacts the online services.
3. **Garden designer** — add beds and objects, drag them into place, and plant them.

## Garden designer
- **Add**: choose an object type in the toolbar. **Move**: drag. **Resize**: drag the orange corner. **Rotate**: R, or the buttons in *Selected object*. Everything snaps to 10 cm unless you untick *Snap*.
- **Zoom**: +/−, Ctrl+mouse wheel, or *Fit*. **Pan**: scroll bars or middle-mouse drag.
- **Undo/redo**: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y (moves, resizes, rotations, adds, deletes, edits). Moves and resizes save automatically; Ctrl+S saves property edits.
- **Plant library** (right panel): search, filter, then drag a plant card onto a bed or press *Add to bed*. The quantity is the number that fit at the species' spacing with 10 cm edge margins; change it freely.
- **Selected object**: name, type, size, rotation, shade estimate (used for sun checks), plantings with status (planned, sown, growing, harvesting, finished), and the **crop rotation check**: choose a plant and date to see whether the bed's history allows it and why. A warning never blocks you; the choice is noted.
- **Year plan** (left panel): pick a month to see sowing windows and expected harvests; beds with activity are outlined in gold and listed. Click an activity to select its bed.
- The status bar shows the cursor position in metres, and the selected object's size, area and rotation. Overlaps with other beds are flagged.

## Next actions
Suggestions from simple rules (window open/closing, harvest due, frost near, low sun, missing seed). Each shows what it was based on. *Add as task* copies it to your task list.

## My library
Add ebooks (button or drag files in). Search finds every word across all books, ignoring accents and capitals; quotes find exact phrases; the last word matches as a prefix. Click a result to read at that passage with the words highlighted. Subjects are listed per book and across the library; click one to filter or search it.
Reader: contents, search in this book, previous/next, text size, night mode. Your place is remembered.

## Settings, backup, language
English/Romanian, light/dark, metric/imperial. *Back up now* makes a verified copy; *Restore…* applies at the next start.

## Keyboard
Ctrl+K / Ctrl+F search everything · Alt+1…5 jump to Dashboard, Tasks, Plants, Seeds, Library · Designer shortcuts as above.
