These books are added to a new library on first start. **Only works that may be redistributed belong here.** Before adding a book, record where it came from and why it may be shared.

## Rules for adding books
- Public domain (check the *country* and the publication date), or a licence that allows redistribution (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC when the app stays free).
- A reproduction made "for personal use" or marked with a buyer's name is **not** redistributable.
- Modern commercial books (even if they are easy to find online) are not.
- Keep individual files small: the installer carries them.
- Users can always add their own books from the Library page; those stay on their own computer.
