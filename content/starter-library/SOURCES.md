# Starter library: sources and licences

These books are added to a new library on first start. **Only works that may be redistributed belong here.** Before adding a book, record where it came from and why it may be shared.

**Rule used:** the author died in 1954 or earlier (so the work is public domain under the "life + 70 years" rule used in the EU, including Romania, as well as in the USA), or the work carries a licence that allows sharing. Project Gutenberg's own label "Public domain in the USA" is *not* enough, because it only covers the USA.

The Project Gutenberg files are the unmodified plain-text downloads and keep their Project Gutenberg licence text. The app skips that header and licence when indexing and reading, so searches only match the book itself.

## Considered and left out
- Rockwell, *Home Vegetable Gardening* (author died 1976) and Burkett, *Agriculture for Beginners* (author died 1962): protected in Romania and the EU.
- Goodrich, *The First Book of Farming* and Piper, *Poultry*: author death year not confirmed.
- Steve Solomon's Gutenberg books: author alive, and the text has no public-domain dedication.
- Historic canning manuals: their methods are unsafe for low-acid foods by current guidance.

## Rules for adding books
- Public domain under the rule above, or a licence that allows redistribution (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC while the app stays free).
- A reproduction made "for personal use" or marked with a buyer's name is **not** redistributable.
- Modern commercial books (even if they are easy to find online) are not.
- Keep individual files small: the installer carries them.
- Users can always add their own books from the Library page; those stay on their own computer.
