# Changelog

## 0.4.0
- **Yield tracking**: record how much you harvested (kg or pieces) from the plant card; the season review shows yield per crop and the best bed.
- **Weather-aware watering** on Today: dry beds from soil sensors (grouped when one sensor serves a whole garden), hot spells, and rain that is coming cancels the chore; one tap logs the watering.
- **Photos** on plant cards (recent pictures of the bed) and a photo gallery in the season review.
- **Planting calendar**: sow, plant out and harvest windows for your crops, from your frost dates; printable.
- **Export / import everything** to one ZIP (records, photos, satellite pictures, your own content, optional book library) to move to another computer.
- **Phone page**: today's list with one-tap actions and photo upload from the phone camera.
- Tidier designer toolbar; "1 day", "1 photo" and similar singular forms.

## 0.3.0
- **Welcome setup** on first start: name and place the homestead (optional online place search), frost dates, animals and crops; the crops become dated sowing to-dos.
- **Photos**: a timeline of pictures of beds, plants, animals and places, with quick add from plant and animal cards on the plan.
- **Reminders**: Windows notifications for urgent things and a daily summary while the app is open; settings and a test button.
- **Season review**: what grew, harvests and problems logged, money, animals, work done, and a crop-rotation suggestion per bed for next year.
- **Print or save as PDF**: the garden plan, building plans, the Today list and the season review.
- **Undo and redo** for building plans (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y) and for tracing, reshaping and deleting places.
- The plan keeps every bed and camera on the ground when places change anywhere in the app.

## 0.2.1
- Raspberry Pi guide opens as its own page from a How-to button in the Greenhouse page, with this PC's address, port, token and place number filled in; available in English and Romanian.
- Plan frame stays aligned when places are traced or reshaped; the plan fits the window when it opens.

## 0.2.0
- **Today** dashboard: late tasks, thirsty plants, eggs and milk to log, treatment withdrawals, greenhouse and garden advice with one-click actions; sun times, saved forecast with weather advice, frost countdown, harvests nearly ready.
- **Building plans**: draw walls, windows and doors, rooms, furniture, greenhouse and workshop items and your own shapes for the house, garage, workshop, barn and greenhouse; indoor plants placed where they stand, with watering alerts.
- Garden designer: plant icons with text on hover only; plant cards (planted, watered, harvest, sensors) with quick logging; animal icons with eggs/milk logging; trace any-shaped building or space on the satellite picture and reshape it later; satellite strength +/-; view the garden as of a past date; soil-sensor dots on beds; settings remembered.
- Raspberry Pi: first-time setup guide in the app and in docs, agent `--check`, `--print-service`, DS18B20 and BME280 support; sensors for outdoor places.
- 18 more plan elements (pergola, patio, cold frame, rain barrel, fire pit and more).

## 0.1.0 — first release
- Garden designer: scaled plan, beds/objects, drag/resize/rotate/snap, undo/redo, zoom/pan, overlap warnings, month planner, plant library with drag-to-bed and spacing-based counts, crop rotation check.
- Command center home screen and per-place pages (notes, dated history of growth, soil and plant treatments and maintenance, plantings, next actions); traced structures and garden spaces appear in the designer; places are searchable.
- Property map: satellite tiles, 3–20 draggable pins, live area/perimeter, outline imported into the designer at true scale. Quick EN/RO switch in the top bar.
- Next-action alerts; sun calculation (offline); opt-in satellite picture and climate/frost derivation.
- Plant encyclopedia (106 starter records (vegetables, herbs, cover crops, fruit and nut trees, berries, vines, cultivated mushrooms for Romania), English/Romanian), companions with evidence levels, planting windows from frost dates.
- Personal library: import EPUB, PDF, MOBI/AZW, FB2, DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML, TXT, Markdown, CBZ; reader; full-text and subject search.
- Tasks, journal, seed inventory, dashboard, backups with verification, restore, light/dark, English/Romanian.
- Installer (Inno Setup) and portable ZIP.
