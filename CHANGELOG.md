# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first release
- Garden designer: scaled plan, beds/objects, drag/resize/rotate/snap, undo/redo, zoom/pan, overlap warnings, month planner, plant library with drag-to-bed and spacing-based counts, crop rotation check.
- Property map: satellite tiles, 3–20 draggable pins, live area/perimeter, outline imported into the designer at true scale. Quick EN/RO switch in the top bar.
- Next-action alerts; sun calculation (offline); opt-in satellite picture and climate/frost derivation.
- Plant encyclopedia (106 starter records (vegetables, herbs, cover crops, fruit and nut trees, berries, vines, cultivated mushrooms for Romania), English/Romanian), companions with evidence levels, planting windows from frost dates.
- Personal library: import EPUB, PDF, MOBI/AZW, FB2, DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML, TXT, Markdown, CBZ; reader; full-text and subject search.
- Tasks, journal, seed inventory, dashboard, backups with verification, restore, light/dark, English/Romanian.
- Installer (Inno Setup) and portable ZIP.
